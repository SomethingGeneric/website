## Google Killing Sideloading
This is very cringe. Android used to differentiate itself based on the principle that it was open source, and you/vendors could extend it however they please.

However, they are now killing sideloading (which, by the way, should just be called "installing software").

# Salvation
The FSF (Free Software Foundation) recently announced their intention to make a fully open source mobile computing setup, by finding existing Android-based devices, and enabling them to boot and use fully open source OS stacks.

Notably, they are NOT intending to go into developing their own whardware.

Read more: https://librephone.fsf.org/ (still under construction as of 10/27/25)
